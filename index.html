How to Use BakerOS

A quick reference guide for every feature

  1. When you first open BakerOS, you'll be prompted to create a Bakery Profile — enter your bakery name and your name, and optionally upload a profile photo.
  2. Once created, you'll land on the Dashboard every time you return.
  3. Need multiple profiles (e.g. personal vs. commercial)? Click Switch Profile at the bottom of the sidebar to add or switch between bakeries.
  1. Go to Ingredients in the sidebar and click + Add Ingredient.
  2. Enter the ingredient name, choose its default unit (g, oz, cup, each, etc.), and set a cost per unit.
  3. Look up price — click the search button next to the cost field to auto-fill the average US retail price from our built-in table of 55+ baking ingredients (flour, butter, eggs, chocolate chips, cake boxes, and more).
  4. For live market prices, add a free Spoonacular API key in Settings → Integrations. The app will query current market data before falling back to the built-in table.
  5. When you change the unit dropdown after a price is auto-filled, the cost recalculates automatically — including cross-type conversions (e.g. lb → cup) using each ingredient's density.
  6. Use the search bar at the top to filter your ingredient list as it grows.
Tip: You can also add ingredients on-the-fly while building a recipe — use the + Add to Library button inside the Recipe Builder.

Saved Recipes are pre-built items you sell repeatedly — a dozen cupcakes, a double-layer cake, a birthday cake pop set. Build them once, reuse them forever.

  1. Go to Saved Recipes → Create Repeat Item.
  2. Enter a recipe name, choose a category (Single Layer Cake, 12 Cupcakes, etc.), and add an optional description.
  3. Upload up to 5 photos of the finished product by clicking or dragging into the photo area.
  4. Add ingredients by dragging them from the left panel into the recipe area. You can also double-click an ingredient to add it. Each added ingredient gets its own amount dial and unit selector.
  5. Use the +/− buttons or mouse wheel on the dials to set exact amounts. Change the unit for each ingredient independently.
  6. Set Time to Create in hours and minutes — this is the full start-to-finish time per batch.
  7. If you make multiple items simultaneously (e.g. 2 cakes in the same oven run), set Simultaneous Items — the app automatically calculates the real per-item time.
  8. The Pricing Preview panel shows your ingredient cost, estimated labor at your hourly rate, markup, and suggested price in real time.
  9. Click Save Recipe when done.
Tip: You can edit any saved recipe later — changes to ingredients or time will immediately reflect in future quotes.
  1. Click New Quote in the sidebar (or the quick action on the Dashboard).
  2. Enter the customer's name, email, phone, and a due date.
  3. Click Add from Saved Recipes to pick one or more of your repeat items. Adjust the quantity for each.
  4. Click Add Custom Item for a one-off item — enter a name, description, and price directly.
  5. Adjust Markup % and Tax % for this quote (defaults come from Settings).
  6. Add any special instructions or notes for the customer in the notes field.
  7. Click Generate Invoice — BakerOS creates a clean, printable invoice with your bakery name, itemized list, and grand total.
  8. From the invoice view, click Print Invoice to save as PDF or print. Click Mark as Completed when the order is fulfilled.
Tip: Invoices are automatically numbered (INV-YYYY-XXXX) and saved to Job History so you always have a record.
  1. Go to Job History to see every quote and invoice you've ever created.
  2. Filter by All / Open / Completed using the tabs at the top.
  3. Use the search bar to find a job by customer name.
  4. Click any row to open the invoice — you can re-print it, or toggle its status between Open and Completed.
  • Profile — Edit your bakery name, owner name, and profile photo.
  • Appearance — Toggle between Light and Dark mode.
  • Default Pricing — Set your hourly labor rate, default markup %, and default tax %. These pre-fill every new quote.
  • Integrations — Add your free Spoonacular API key to enable live market price lookups. Get a free key →
  • Time Categories — Customize estimated prep times by product type. These feed into the pricing preview in the recipe builder.
  • Units — Set your default unit system (Imperial or Metric).
  • Delete Profile — Permanently removes your profile and all associated data. This cannot be undone.

BakerOS calculates a suggested price for every recipe using three components:

  • Ingredient cost — sum of all ingredients × their amount × cost per unit in your library.
  • Labor cost — your hourly rate × hours per item (accounting for simultaneous items).
  • Markup — a percentage added on top to cover overhead, packaging, and profit.

The price lookup feature sources ingredient costs automatically:

  • Without an API key: uses a built-in table of average US retail prices for 55+ baking ingredients.
  • With a Spoonacular API key: queries live market data first, then falls back to the built-in table.
  • Prices auto-convert between units of the same type (oz ↔ lb, tsp ↔ cup). Cross-type conversions (oz ↔ cup) use per-ingredient density data.
  • A ~ estimated badge appears on any auto-filled price — you can always override it manually.
Note: All prices are estimates. Actual costs vary by region, season, and supplier. Review and adjust ingredient costs periodically to keep your quotes accurate.
  • Mouse wheel on dials — scroll over any amount spinner to increase or decrease the value without clicking.
  • Double-click ingredients — in the Recipe Builder, double-click an ingredient chip to add it to the recipe (alternative to drag & drop).
  • Quick add from builder — click + Add to Library inside the Recipe Builder to create a new ingredient without leaving the recipe.
  • Dark mode — toggle in Settings → Appearance, or it follows your last saved preference per profile.
  • Multiple profiles — perfect for managing separate business lines or a shared tablet. Each profile has completely independent ingredients, recipes, jobs, and settings.
  • Print invoices — the print view hides all navigation and UI chrome, giving you a clean document ready for PDF export.
  • All data is local — BakerOS stores everything in your browser. No account, no server, no subscription. Clear browser data carefully if you want to reset.
